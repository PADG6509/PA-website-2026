UV-Vis spectrophotometer for water and wastewater analysis

NANOCOLOR Advance

The NANOCOLOR Advance spectrophotometer combines the key features of the PF-12Plus portable compact photometer and the high-precision NANOCOLOR VIS II spectrophotometer. It delivers precise and reliable measurement results in everyday laboratory routine, and thanks to its rechargeable battery it can also be used on the move. With a wavelength range of 340–800 nm, it can measure all photometrically evaluable MACHEREY-NAGEL test kits, which makes NANOCOLOR Advance a universal instrument for the full range of water and wastewater analysis.

Atomic force microscope for high-end research

DriveAFM

DriveAFM, Nanosurf's revolutionary tip-scanning AFM system, gives researchers outstanding performance across a wide range of applications, whatever the type of sample being analysed. Its unique ultra-low-noise architecture enables high-resolution data acquisition and opens up a far wider range of research possibilities. The system is compatible with high-precision small cantilevers, which further improve overall measurement performance.

DriveAFM can operate as a stand-alone system or integrated with an inverted optical microscope. A full range of imaging modes, support for small cantilevers and numerous accessories and options covering almost every research need make DriveAFM a future-proof solution and the ideal choice for demanding, top-level researchers. The system is fully motorised, enabling a high degree of automation and making experiment setup and execution considerably easier.

Nanosurf Studio, the advanced control software, was designed from the ground up to meet the needs of experienced researchers while making AFM technology more intuitively accessible to less experienced users.

Flexible mid-range atomic force microscope

FlexAFM

The Nanosurf FlexAFM is recognised worldwide as an ideal tool for researchers in biology, physics and materials science. This atomic force microscope is designed to be flexible, configurable and easy to use, which has made it the preferred choice of hundreds of researchers across many disciplines. FlexAFM integrates easily with an inverted optical microscope for biological applications, or can be installed in a glovebox for 2D materials research. A wide range of operating modes and accessories allows the system to be tailored to the specific requirements of each experiment.

FlexAFM is a versatile system that can be configured for different research needs – as a stand-alone system, in a glovebox configuration or combined with an inverted optical microscope. With its many options, accessories and imaging modes, it is an ideal choice for researchers from a variety of scientific fields. The system combines a compact, lightweight design with robustness and intuitive operation. Quick switching between sample holders and operating modes, easy cantilever mounting thanks to an intuitive alignment system, and an integrated camera with top and side views make work and sample navigation easier.

FlexAFM is a reliable, flexible and affordable mid-range AFM that stands out for its exceptional adaptability and ease of use.
