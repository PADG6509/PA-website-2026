Laboratory Furniture Presentation, Podgorica 2024
Download materials

What sets a partnership apart is that every first attempt isn't really an attempt at all — it's a successful first step!

And so, on 23.10.2024, we took the first step into a new market presence in Montenegro, together with our partners and friends from Danlab Ltd.We brought together many long-standing as well as new associates and, in a wonderful setting, told our story: the presentation of the laboratory furniture programme jointly represented by Danlab Ltd. and PROANALYTICA DOO SRBIJA aimed to remind the market of our many years of experience and way of working, while also introducing the importance of a professional and correct approach to laboratory work.

It was important to us that you gained a clear picture of how you can rely on us — and together, we succeeded in that.

A big thank you to everyone who heard us out!

Total Workflow Workshop 2024
Download materials

Over the three days of the "Total Workflow" workshop, more than 70 guests visited our demo laboratory. Together with Valerio Rindone, we brought the Milestone - Helping Chemists concept to life through live equipment performance demonstrations, with active participation from all our guests.

On this occasion, Dr. Ralf Winkler and Alexander Turowski, representatives of our long-standing partner Elementar who were guests at the workshop, announced a new workshop project for next year.

Thank you to everyone who attended — we hope to see you again soon!
