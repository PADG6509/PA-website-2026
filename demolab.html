Green Supercritical Fluid Extraction Workshop 2024
Materials

In Proanalytica’s demo laboratory in Zemun, the Green Supercritical Fluid Extraction Workshop was successfully held on 28–29 May, organised by Proanalytica and Superex Supercritical.

Over the two days, more than 40 participants from faculties and institutes attended the workshop. The theoretical part focused on an innovative, “green” technology that is most often used as an alternative to toxic organic solvents.

The practical work was carried out on the Superex SC-1000 with a 1-litre extractor, using scCO₂ as the fluid to extract lavender flowers at a pressure of 150 bar and a temperature of 45 °C. Participants learned how to optimise the key parameters to make the extraction as efficient as possible.

The SC-1000 supercritical extraction system is located in our demo laboratory, and we invite you to get to know this innovative extraction technique.

Materials
