Built around the instruments we trust.

Since 2000, Proanalytica has supplied laboratory equipment, analytical instruments, chemicals and consumables from Belgrade — and manufactured fume cupboards and laboratory furniture in-house.

What we do

From instrument selection to the reagent on the shelf.

Proanalytica is a distributor specialised in laboratory equipment, analytical instruments, laboratory chemicals and consumables, and also a manufacturer of fume cupboards and laboratory furniture. Our commitment to top-level quality of the goods offered, prompt service and application support guarantees reliable analyses, long-term utilisation of equipment, and a favourable return on investment. Proanalytica is part of the international Lach-Ner group of companies, manufacturers and distributors of laboratory equipment and consumables.

How the Lach:ner Group works
