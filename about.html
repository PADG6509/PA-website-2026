Built around the instruments we trust

Since 2000, Proanalytica has supplied laboratory equipment, analytical instruments, chemicals and consumables from Belgrade — and manufactured fume cupboards and laboratory furniture in-house.

What we do

Consulting • Planning • Sales • Support • Training

Proanalytica is a distributor specialised in laboratory equipment, analytical instruments, laboratory chemicals and consumables, and also a manufacturer of fume cupboards and laboratory furniture. Our commitment to top-level quality of the goods offered, prompt service and application support guarantees reliable analyses, long-term utilisation of equipment, and a favourable return on investment. Proanalytica is part of the international Lach-Ner Group, a renowned manufacturer of laboratory chemicals.

Beyond work, we strengthen our internal cooperation through shared activities outside the office, and this makes us a closer and stronger team!

Milestones

Twenty-six years, six turning points

2000

Proanalytica started with laboratory chemicals distribution.

2001

2006

The Laboratory Furniture Department started production.

2012

2022

How the Lach:ner Group works

Precision

We treat specifications, certificates and shelf lives as seriously as the labs we supply do — because a result is only as good as what went into it.

Continuity

A method that works shouldn't break because a consumable is out of stock. We plan deliveries around your protocols, not the other way round.

Straight answers

If a product isn't the right fit for your application, we'll say so. Long-term relationships matter more to us than a single order.
