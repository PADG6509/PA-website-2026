Laboratory Chemicals

A complete range for everyday lab work

As part of the Lach:ner Group, we supply chemicals for every stage of laboratory work – from p.a.-grade chemicals, solvents and acids for AAS/ICP to certified reference materials and analytical standards for calibration, method validation and accreditation. Alongside Lach:ner chemicals, the range includes products from renowned manufacturers such as PanReac AppliChem, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Alfa Aesar, LGC Standards, CPAchem and LIPOMED, as well as MACHEREY-NAGEL general and chromatography consumables.

Laboratory Furniture

Designed and manufactured in-house

We have been designing and manufacturing laboratory furniture and fume hoods in-house since 2006. Every laboratory is fitted out to measure – from benches and fume hoods to safety cabinets for flammable and aggressive substances, neutralization systems, extraction arms and laboratory hoods – so that the space, the workflow and the safety requirements fit together as one whole.

Laboratory Equipment

Instruments you can see and try

Analytical instruments and sample preparation equipment from leading European and global manufacturers such as Milestone, Elementar, Systec, LC Tech, Nanosurf and MACHEREY-NAGEL. The range covers elemental analysis, microwave digestion and extraction, sample preparation, sterilization, spectrophotometry and microscopy, and climate chambers. Many of the instruments can be seen and tried in our DEMOLAB demo laboratory in Zemun.
