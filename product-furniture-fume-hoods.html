Fume Hoods

Fume hoods are a key safety factor when working in chemical laboratories. They are designed to remove harmful vapours from the working environment, and every element of their design serves that purpose.

The construction of the fume hood and the materials used are purpose-selected to ensure its long-term, reliable operation.

Benchtop fume hood

Economic PRO

Economic PRO is a benchtop fume hood from our own production, with a lightweight construction and chemical-resistant protection of the entire working area. This model is easy to relocate, yet still offers a wide range of standard and optional equipment: water, power and gas supply, a UV lamp and more. The fume hood can be fitted into your existing laboratory setup, but it is often supplied with a matching bench that we build to our own standard specifically for laboratory use, using proven materials from our range. If your work does not involve high thermal loads or a high capacity for extracting harmful vapours, this is the right choice for your laboratory!

Fume hood for the most demanding processes

Premium PRO

The “Premium Pro” model is a fume hood of the highest quality. It has been part of our production from the very start and features an all-steel construction with anti-corrosion epoxy coating, work-chamber linings with laboratory-grade resistance to moisture, acid vapours and high temperatures, and worktops of monolithic ceramic or compact laminate (HPL). Its flexible design allows non-standard fume hood dimensions and applications. This fume hood is intended for the most demanding laboratory processes and offers high operational reliability thanks to the materials we use in production. All materials in our range come with documentation giving you insight into their properties and intended use, so that everything can be matched to your requirements.

Under-bench units include standard wooden cabinets as well as a range of safety cabinets for storing flammable substances, acids and/or bases, collecting waste liquids, or storing laboratory glassware.

Digital control, EN 14175-1

Premium PRO Plus

The “Premium Pro Plus” model is a premium-quality fume hood from our production line. Reinforced construction, upgraded electronics, digital control of the fume hood and display of the airflow inside the working area in accordance with EN 14175-1. Comfortable and safe operation: this model also has audio/visual alarms in case of operating faults. The fume hood is certified and holds the approved 3A (AAA) mark, and as such fully complies with all domestic regulations on technical equipment placed on the market. A wide choice of materials and optional equipment for every application makes this fume hood a suitable choice for any type of laboratory.

Under-bench units include standard wooden cabinets as well as a range of safety cabinets for storing flammable substances, acids and/or bases, collecting waste liquids, or storing laboratory glassware.
