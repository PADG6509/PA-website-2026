Basic inorganic and organic chemicals

Lach:ner

Lach:ner is a progressive European company that has built on the solid foundations of its long tradition in chemical manufacturing to develop expertise in customising pure and speciality chemicals. Alongside its constant attention to the product and its quality, the company listens carefully to the changing needs of its customers and responds by adapting its range, services and production procedures. It sees change as an opportunity to develop new solutions, new products and new applications, and to add value for the end customer.

The range comprises a purpose-selected portfolio of basic inorganic and organic chemicals under the Lach-Ner brand. The chemicals are primarily intended for laboratory, research, medical, industrial and educational applications, as well as other purposes. The product range is continuously improved, taking customers' requirements and ideas into account.
